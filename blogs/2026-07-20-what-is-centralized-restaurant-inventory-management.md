Running multiple restaurant locations means keeping track of far more than just recipes and reservations. If you've ever found yourself calling one location to ask about their tomato prices while another unit runs out of chicken mid-service, you know how quickly things can spiral when inventory management lives in silos. Restaurant inventory management becomes exponentially more complex with each new location you add to your portfolio.
